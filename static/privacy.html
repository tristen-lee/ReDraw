Privacy

You don't need an account to search, and Cupholder doesn't use Google Analytics, ad trackers, or cookies from anyone else.

What gets recorded

To see whether the site is actually useful, Cupholder keeps its own usage log: which pages are opened, the areas people draw and any keyword typed with them, which listings get opened, and clicks through to apply. Each visit is tagged with a random ID your browser makes up and stores locally, so a return visit can be counted as the same person. That ID isn't connected to your name, email, or anything else about you.

The usage log doesn't include your IP address, your device's location, or anything you type outside the search box. A drawn area is stored as a shape, not as where you are. The web host keeps ordinary server logs for a short time, as every site's host does.

Posting a job

Employers who post give a contact email. It's used to send the link for managing the post and to ask about the listing. It isn't shown publicly, sold, or shared.

Clearing it

Clearing this site's data in your browser drops the random ID, and you'll show up as a new visitor. To ask about anything else, email tristen@cupholder.work.
