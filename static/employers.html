Your opening, on the map where the work is.

Cupholder is a job board for Columbia County and the Portland area. Nobody here searches a radius. They draw the drive they'd actually make, and your job shows up if it's on the way.

Post a job for free or email Tristen

What it costs

Nothing. No account, no contract, no fee per applicant.

Where applicants go

Straight to you, through your application link or your inbox. Cupholder doesn't collect résumés or stand between you and the person applying.

Where your job shows up

As a pin at your street address, so people can see exactly how far the drive is. Every posting also gets its own page set up so Google's job search can list it.

How posting works

  1. Fill out one form. Job title, worksite address, what the job is, and how to apply. About five minutes.
  2. I look it over. Every post gets read by a person before it goes up, usually within a day.
  3. It goes on the map for 30 days. Pinned at the address you gave.
  4. You get a link by email. Use it to keep the post up longer or take it down once you've hired someone.
